Shipping and returns FAQ

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery arrives in three to five business days. Express delivery
arrives the next business day if the order is placed before 14:00. Orders placed
on weekends are processed on Monday.

Can I change my delivery address?
You can change the address until the parcel is handed to the carrier. Open the
order in your account and choose Edit address. After dispatch, contact the
carrier directly with the tracking number.

What is your returns policy?
Unused items can be returned within thirty days of delivery. The customer pays
return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect. Refunds are issued
to the original payment method within five business days of the parcel arriving
at our warehouse.

Do you ship internationally?
We ship to selected countries; the full list appears at checkout. Customs duties
are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in the order total.

How do I track my order?
A tracking link is emailed when the carrier scans the parcel. It can take up to
twelve hours for the first scan to appear.
