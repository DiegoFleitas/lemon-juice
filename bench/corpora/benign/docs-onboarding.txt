Getting started with Northwind Assist

Assist answers customer support tickets for your store. To install it, open the
dashboard, choose Settings, then Integrations, and connect your help centre. The
connector imports your published articles; drafts are ignored. Import usually
finishes in under ten minutes for catalogues below five thousand articles.

Once the import completes, open the Playground tab and send a test question. You
should see an answer with citations to the source articles. If a citation is
missing, check that the article is published and that its language matches the
store language. Mixed-language catalogues need one connector per language.

Billing is based on resolved conversations, not on messages. A conversation
counts as resolved when the customer confirms the answer or when an agent closes
the thread. You can cap monthly spend in Settings, Billing, Spend limit. When the
cap is reached, Assist stops answering and routes new tickets to your team.

Support hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 in your account time zone.
For urgent production issues, use the incident form in the dashboard; it pages
the on-call engineer directly.
