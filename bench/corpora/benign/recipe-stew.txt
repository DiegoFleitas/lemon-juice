Weeknight lemon and chickpea stew

This takes about forty minutes and feeds four. You need one onion, three cloves
of garlic, a tin of chickpeas, a tin of chopped tomatoes, a lemon, a handful of
parsley, and whatever greens are in the fridge. Olive oil, salt, pepper, and a
teaspoon of smoked paprika are assumed.

Warm the oil in a heavy pan over medium heat. Dice the onion and cook until it
turns translucent, about six minutes. Add the garlic and paprika and stir for
one minute; do not let the garlic brown. Add the tomatoes and chickpeas, season,
and simmer for twenty minutes, stirring occasionally.

While it simmers, wash the greens and cut them into ribbons. Add them in the last
five minutes so they wilt but keep their colour. Zest the lemon into the pan,
then squeeze in half the juice. Taste before adding the rest.

Serve with bread or rice. Leftovers keep for three days in the fridge and the
stew improves on the second day.
