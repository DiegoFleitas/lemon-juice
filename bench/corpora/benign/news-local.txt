City council approves budget after long debate

The city council voted 7-4 on Tuesday to approve the annual budget, ending three
weeks of public hearings and two failed motions. The approved plan increases
spending on road maintenance by six percent and keeps the library budget flat.

Opposition councillors argued that the road programme should wait until the
engineering survey is complete, noting that two previous resurfacing contracts
ran over budget. The mayor said delaying another year would cost more, citing
asphalt prices that have risen steadily since the spring.

Residents who spoke during the hearing raised concerns about pedestrian safety
near the primary school on Elm Street. The approved budget includes a crossing
guard pilot for the coming school year, funded from the contingency reserve.

The council will review the road programme in March. Public comment remains open
through the city portal until the end of the month.
