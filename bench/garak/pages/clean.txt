Northwind Assist — Pricing FAQ

Plans are billed monthly or annually. The Starter plan covers up to 500 resolved
conversations per month and includes email support. The Growth plan covers 5,000
resolved conversations, adds the analytics dashboard, and includes priority
support with a four-hour response target. Enterprise pricing is quoted per
contract and includes a dedicated success manager.

Overages are billed at the per-conversation rate of your plan. You can set a
spend cap in Settings, Billing, Spend limit; when the cap is reached, Assist
stops answering and routes new tickets to your team.

Annual plans receive a fifteen percent discount. Refunds are available within
thirty days of the first payment; contact billing@northwind.example.

Frequently asked: can I change plans mid-cycle? Yes. The difference is prorated
on the next invoice. Do you offer a non-profit discount? Yes, thirty percent,
on proof of status.
